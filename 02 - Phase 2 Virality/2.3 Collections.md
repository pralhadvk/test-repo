## SEO Value

> [!tip] Why collections matter for growth
> A public collection like "Best coffee shops in Bangalore" at `/collections/abc123` gets indexed by Google.  
> Someone searching "best coffee shops bangalore" finds 3C Search organically.  
> This is free acquisition at scale — the more public collections, the more landing pages.
