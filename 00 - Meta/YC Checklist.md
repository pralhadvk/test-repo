## YC Standard Questions

### What does your company do?
3C Search returns the top 3 AI-curated results for any query — fast, shareable, opinionated. We replace the wall of links with a ranked, explainable answer you can send to anyone.

### What's the market size?
Search is a $200B+ market. We're targeting the "quick answer" segment — people who want a recommendation, not a research session. Adjacent to ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google's AI Overview.

### Why now?
LLMs just became fast and cheap enough to serve structured search results at scale. GPT-4o-mini costs ~$0.0001 per query. The UX shift from links → AI answers is happening now.
