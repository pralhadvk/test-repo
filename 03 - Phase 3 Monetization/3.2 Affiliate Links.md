## Affiliate Map

| Category | Program | Link Pattern |
|----------|---------|-------------|
| Products / Tech | Amazon Associates | `amazon.com/s?k={name}&tag={id}` |
| Travel (hotels) | Booking.com | `booking.com/search?ss={name}&aid={id}` |
| Travel (flights) | Skyscanner | `skyscanner.net/...&ref={id}` |
| Apps | App Store / Play Store | Direct link (no affiliate, but good UX) |
| Entertainment | JustWatch | `justwatch.com/us/search?q={name}` |

---

## Revenue Estimate

- Amazon Associates: 3–8% commission, avg order $40 → ~$1.50/conversion
- Booking.com: 4% commission, avg booking $200 → ~$8/conversion
- Even 10 bookings/month from Booking.com = $80 MRR in affiliate revenue
